Reports a step that more than one step definition matches.

Cucumber refuses to guess: a step matched by two definitions fails the scenario with AmbiguousStepDefinitionsException. Without this inspection the problem is invisible until the suite runs, because the IDE quietly resolves such a step to whichever definition has the longest pattern.

Usually one of the two patterns is meant to be narrower than it is — an unanchored regular expression, or a {word} where a literal was intended.
